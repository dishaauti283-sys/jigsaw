About the Game

Where Speed Meets Strategy

Corlesce fuses the high-octane world of Formula 1 with the calm mental workout of jigsaw puzzles. Reconstruct legendary race moments, iconic circuits, and legendary machines — one piece at a time.

Whether you're a die-hard motorsport fan or a casual puzzle solver, Corlesce delivers a uniquely satisfying challenge. Shift your brain into top gear and set a new lap record.
